# Solo schedule (percent of total hack time)

- 0–10%: Read rules, choose track, narrow ideas.
- 10–20%: Prototype core integration or most uncertain component.
- 20–55%: Ship one end-to-end vertical slice with a local test.
- 55–70%: Polish UX and make result visually obvious.
- 70–80%: QA, fallback and smoke testing.
- 80–100%: Freeze features; demo rehearsal, pitch, README, recording (if allowed), submission.

Cut scope when behind. Sleep and breaks are not optional if multi-day fatigue could make the demo less reliable.
